Add to Home Screen — explicit steps

iOS Safari never auto-prompts to install a PWA. You must invoke Share → Add to Home Screen manually. If the row isn't there, one of these is wrong:

  1. You opened the URL from a non-Safari app (Slack, Mail, Notes, etc.). Open it in Safari first.
  2. The page wasn't fully loaded yet. Wait until you see this section before tapping Share.
  3. You're using Chrome / Firefox / Edge on iOS — they don't support A2HS. Use Safari.
  4. You're in Private Browsing. Turn it off (Safari hides A2HS in private mode).
  5. The Share icon isn't on the address bar — it's at the bottom-center of the screen on iPhone, a square with an arrow pointing up.

Steps that work:

  1. In Safari, tap the Share icon (square + up arrow, bottom-center).
  2. Scroll the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Confirm the name "Binaural Therapy" and tap Add.
  4. Open the app from the Home Screen (not from Safari tab) — that's where background audio matters.

iOS Validation Checklist

  1. Tap Start. Status pills turn green.
  2. Tap Play binaural. You hear a soft delta beat.
  3. Lock the screen. Audio should continue for ≥10 s (appspec §8.1, audioSession.type = "playback" + anchor pattern).
  4. Pull down Control Center / lock screen. You should see a Now Playing card labeled "Binaural Therapy — Spike".
  5. Flip the hardware Ring/Silent switch to silent. Audio should still be audible (appspec §8.2 — type = "playback" bypass).
  6. Use the on-lock-screen Pause / Play button. Pause for 30 s, then play. If it fails: known bug, Apple Forums 762582 (appspec §8.4).
  7. Swipe the app away from the app switcher, re-tap the icon. Audio context should resume via the visibilitychange handler.
  8. Watch the Live Status block — AudioContext state should cycle suspended → running as you background/foreground.
